## Personal Profile
I conduct computer graphics R&D related to digital characters used in film CG VFX and animation production. I primarily develop in-house solutions related to motion capture and character rigging, as well as machine learning-based solutions to streamline and automate production workflows.

## Interests
- Motion Capture / Facial Capture and Retargeting
- Mesh Scanning and Facial Rigging for High-Quality Facial Production
- Machine Learning-based Production Automation and Efficiency
